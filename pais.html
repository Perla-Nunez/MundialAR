Compatibilidad & FFmpeg (ayuda)

H.264 soporte: — · AAC soporte: —

Combo video/mp4; codecs="avc1.42E01E, mp4a.40.2": —

FFmpeg “web-ready” (MP4 H.264/AAC + faststart):

ffmpeg -i input.any \
  -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 22 -profile:v high -level 4.1 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -c:a aac -b:a 160k \
  -movflags +faststart \
  output-web.mp4

Forzar 1080p @30fps:

ffmpeg -i input.any -vf "scale=-2:1080,fps=30" \
  -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 22 -profile:v high -level 4.1 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -c:a aac -b:a 160k -movflags +faststart output-1080p.mp4

Solo re-empaquetar (si ya es H.264 + AAC):

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -c copy -movflags +faststart output-faststart.mp4

Servidor/Hosting:

  • Content-Type: video/mp4
  • Accept-Ranges: bytes (permite “seek”)
  • Si sirves desde otro dominio, habilita CORS (Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *)
